About

I run the company that builds the software.

I started Juleybib Limited in 2020, while I was studying software engineering. It's small — two people — which means when you hire me you're talking to the person who scopes the work and the person who writes the code. There's no account manager relaying messages and no handoff to a junior once the contract is signed.

Alongside that I spent two months in 2024 as a React Native developer for Inventify, a remote team in the UAE, building and maintaining mobile apps for iOS and Android.

I work in English, Swahili and Arabic, and enough Hindi to be useful. Being in UTC+3 means a full working day of overlap with the Gulf and East Africa, and mornings with Europe.
